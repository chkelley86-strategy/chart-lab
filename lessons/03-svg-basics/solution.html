03 · SVG basics (solution)

Charts are drawn in SVG: shapes placed on an x/y grid. The top-left corner is (0, 0), and y gets bigger as you go down. Open exercise.js.

1. Draw shapes

Use d3 to append a rectangle, a circle, a line and some text to the SVG below (400 × 200).

2. Groups and margins

Add a g shifted in by a margin, so everything inside starts at the margin, not the edge.

3. Your first bar chart, by hand
